Will You Get In?

Describe a graduate school application and a fine-tuned DistilBERT model will predict whether it was accepted or rejected. It was trained on about 31,000 self-reported results from GradCafe, and gets 79% of held-out results right.

This is a portfolio demo, not admissions advice. The model only sees what people chose to post on GradCafe. It knows nothing about essays, letters, research fit or how many places a program has. It also leans heavily on degree type: about 31% of PhD results in the test data were acceptances, but the model predicts only 19% of them as accepted (for Master’s results: 76% actual, 85% predicted). Please don’t base any real decision on it.
